About CocoaLuxe

Our Story

Founded in 2017 by chocolatiers who fell in love with single-origin beans, CocoaLuxe began as a weekend experiment and grew into a cherished neighborhood atelier. Our philosophy blends classic techniques with contemporary flavors — think saffron ganache, tamarind caramel, and spiced mango truffles.

Sourcing & Sustainability

We partner directly with smallholder farms in Ghana and Ecuador. Our direct-trade model ensures fair pay and traceability — each limited-edition bar includes a QR code linking to the farm story.
